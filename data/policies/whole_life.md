# Lumina LifeLegacy (Whole Life Plan)

Lumina Assurance is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic sample data for a demo project.

## 1. Overview
Lumina LifeLegacy is a participating whole life plan that covers the life assured for life and builds cash value over time. Policyholders may receive non-guaranteed bonuses from Lumina's participating fund.

## 2. Premium Payment Terms
Premiums can be paid over 5, 10, 15 or 20 years, or up to age 65. Cover continues for life after premiums stop.

## 3. Benefits
- Death benefit: the sum assured plus any bonuses attached to the policy.
- Total and permanent disability benefit before age 70: the sum assured plus bonuses.
- Cash value: available after the policy has been in force for at least 2 years.

## 4. Bonuses
Reversionary bonuses, once declared, become guaranteed. Terminal bonuses are paid on death, surrender or maturity and are not guaranteed. Bonus rates depend on the performance of the participating fund and may change.

## 5. Policy Loans
After the policy has a cash value, the policyholder can borrow up to 80% of the cash value. Interest is charged on the loan at a rate set by Lumina, currently 5.5% a year. If the loan plus interest exceeds the cash value, the policy will end.

## 6. Surrender
The policyholder may surrender the policy for its cash value. Surrendering in the early years usually returns much less than the premiums paid.

## 7. Free-Look Period
The policyholder may cancel within 14 days of receiving the policy documents for a full refund of premiums paid, less medical examination costs.

## 8. Claims
Claims require a claim form, a death certificate or TPD medical report, and the claimant's identity document.
