# Lumina HospitalCare Plus (Hospitalisation Plan)

Lumina Assurance is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic sample data for a demo project.

## 1. Overview
Lumina HospitalCare Plus pays for hospital and surgical bills, both inpatient and day surgery, at private or public hospitals, subject to the plan limits and deductible.

## 2. Plan Types
- Plan A: private hospital, any ward class. Annual limit S$1,500,000.
- Plan B: public hospital, up to A-class ward. Annual limit S$1,000,000.
- Plan C: public hospital, up to B1-class ward. Annual limit S$500,000.

## 3. Deductible and Co-insurance
The yearly deductible is S$3,500 for Plans A and B and S$2,500 for Plan C. After the deductible, the policyholder pays 10% co-insurance. The optional HospitalCare Rider covers 95% of the deductible and co-insurance, capping the policyholder's co-payment at S$3,000 per year for treatment by a panel doctor.

## 4. Pre- and Post-Hospitalisation
Medical expenses incurred up to 90 days before admission and up to 180 days after discharge are covered, if they relate to the same condition. For non-panel doctors, the post-hospitalisation period is 90 days.

## 5. Pre-existing Conditions
Pre-existing conditions declared at application may be excluded or covered with a loading after underwriting. Undeclared pre-existing conditions are not covered.

## 6. Waiting Period
There is no waiting period for accidents. For illnesses, a 30-day waiting period applies from the policy start date. For specified conditions (hernia, cataract, haemorrhoids and benign growths), a 12-month waiting period applies.

## 7. Exclusions
Not covered: cosmetic surgery, pregnancy and childbirth (except listed complications), dental treatment unless caused by an accident, treatment for alcohol or drug abuse, and experimental treatment.

## 8. Claims and Letter of Guarantee
For admissions to panel hospitals, Lumina can issue a Letter of Guarantee so the patient pays no deposit. Claims for reimbursement must be submitted within 90 days of discharge with itemised bills and the discharge summary.
