# Lumina SmartSaver 10 (Endowment Savings Plan)

Lumina Assurance is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic sample data for a demo project.

## 1. Overview
Lumina SmartSaver 10 is a participating endowment plan with a 10-year policy term. Premiums are paid for the first 5 years. It is designed for medium-term savings goals such as education or a home down payment.

## 2. Eligibility
Entry age is 0 (at least 15 days old) to 70. The minimum annual premium is S$1,200.

## 3. Maturity Benefit
At the end of year 10, the plan pays the guaranteed maturity value plus any non-guaranteed bonuses. The guaranteed maturity value is 101% of total premiums paid. At an illustrated investment return of 4.25% a year, the projected total maturity value is about 118% of total premiums paid. Illustrated values are not guaranteed.

## 4. Death Benefit
If the life assured dies during the policy term, Lumina pays the higher of 101% of total premiums paid or the cash value, plus any bonuses.

## 5. Capital Guarantee
Capital is guaranteed only at maturity. Surrendering the policy before maturity may return less than the total premiums paid, especially in the first 5 years.

## 6. Premium Holiday and Non-payment
After the 5-year premium term ends, no further premiums are due. If a premium is missed during the premium term, a 30-day grace period applies. After that, the policy may lapse unless it has enough cash value for an automatic premium loan.

## 7. Free-Look Period
The policyholder may cancel within 14 days of receiving the policy documents for a full refund of premiums paid.

## 8. Claims and Payouts
Maturity payouts are credited by bank transfer within 7 working days of the maturity date, provided Lumina has the policyholder's bank details.
