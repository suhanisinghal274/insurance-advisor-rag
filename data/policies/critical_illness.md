# Lumina CriticalCare 37 (Critical Illness Plan)

Lumina Assurance is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic sample data for a demo project.

## 1. Overview
Lumina CriticalCare 37 is a standalone critical illness plan. It pays a lump sum on diagnosis of one of 37 covered critical illnesses at the severe stage, such as major cancer, heart attack of specified severity and stroke with permanent neurological deficit.

## 2. Eligibility
Entry age is 1 to 60. Coverage lasts to age 75 or 85, chosen at application. The minimum sum assured is S$25,000.

## 3. Benefits
- Critical illness benefit: 100% of the sum assured on diagnosis of a covered illness at the severe stage. The policy then ends.
- Early-stage benefit: 25% of the sum assured, capped at S$100,000, on diagnosis of a covered illness at an early stage. This is paid once per illness and reduces the remaining sum assured.
- Death benefit: S$5,000 if the life assured dies before any critical illness benefit is paid.

## 4. Waiting Period
A 90-day waiting period applies from the policy start date for major cancer, heart attack, coronary artery bypass surgery, other serious coronary artery disease and angioplasty. No benefit is paid for these illnesses if signs, symptoms or diagnosis occur within the waiting period.

## 5. Survival Period
The life assured must survive 7 days after diagnosis for the critical illness benefit to be paid.

## 6. Exclusions
Not covered: illnesses caused by pre-existing conditions, self-inflicted injury, HIV infection (except where acquired through a blood transfusion or as an occupational hazard), and illnesses diagnosed outside the waiting-period rules above.

## 7. Premiums
Premiums are not guaranteed and may be revised based on claims experience, with 30 days' written notice. The policy has no cash value.

## 8. Claims
A claim needs a claim form and a diagnosis confirmed by a registered specialist with supporting test results, such as histopathology for cancer.
