# Lumina TermShield (Term Life Insurance)

Lumina Assurance is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic sample data for a demo project.

## 1. Overview
Lumina TermShield is a term life plan that pays a lump sum if the life assured dies or becomes terminally ill during the policy term. It has no cash value and does not pay anything if the life assured survives to the end of the term.

## 2. Eligibility
Entry age is 18 to 65 (age next birthday). The policy term can be 10, 20 or 30 years, or up to age 75, whichever ends first. The minimum sum assured is S$100,000 and the maximum without full medical underwriting is S$2,000,000.

## 3. Benefits
- Death benefit: 100% of the sum assured.
- Terminal illness benefit: 100% of the sum assured, paid in advance if a specialist certifies that death is expected within 12 months. Once paid, the policy ends.
- Total and permanent disability (TPD) benefit: 100% of the sum assured if TPD happens before age 70.

## 4. Riders
- Critical Illness Accelerator rider: pays up to 100% of the sum assured early on diagnosis of one of 37 covered critical illnesses. Any payment reduces the death benefit by the same amount.
- Waiver of Premium rider: future premiums are waived if the policyholder is diagnosed with a covered critical illness.

## 5. Premiums
Premiums are level and guaranteed for the first 10 years, then reviewed every 5 years. Premiums can be paid monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly. A grace period of 30 days applies to each premium after the due date; the policy stays in force during the grace period.

## 6. Exclusions
No death benefit is payable if death is due to suicide within 1 year of the policy start date or of the latest reinstatement. In that case Lumina refunds the premiums paid, without interest. TPD caused by self-inflicted injury, war, or participation in a criminal act is not covered.

## 7. Free-Look Period
The policyholder may cancel the policy within 14 days of receiving the policy documents and receive a full refund of premiums paid, less any medical examination costs.

## 8. Claims
Death claims should be submitted within 6 months of the date of death. Required documents: claim form, death certificate, the policyholder's identity document and, if requested, medical reports. Lumina aims to decide on complete claims within 10 working days.
