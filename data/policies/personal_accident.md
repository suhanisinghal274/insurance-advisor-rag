# Lumina AccidentGuard (Personal Accident Plan)

Lumina Assurance is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic sample data for a demo project.

## 1. Overview
Lumina AccidentGuard is a yearly renewable personal accident plan that covers accidental death, permanent disablement and medical expenses from accidents worldwide.

## 2. Eligibility
Entry age is 16 to 70. The plan renews every year up to age 75.

## 3. Benefits
- Accidental death: S$200,000.
- Permanent disablement: up to S$200,000, as a percentage set out in the disablement schedule.
- Accidental medical expenses: up to S$3,000 per accident, including treatment by a Traditional Chinese Medicine physician up to S$300 per accident.
- Daily hospital income: S$100 per day of hospitalisation due to an accident, up to 90 days.

## 4. Infectious Disease Benefit
Hospitalisation due to dengue fever, Zika virus or hand, foot and mouth disease is covered under the daily hospital income benefit.

## 5. Exclusions
Not covered: injuries from professional sports, motor racing, war, being under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and self-inflicted injury. Injuries from scuba diving deeper than 30 metres are not covered.

## 6. Premiums
Premiums do not depend on age, but depend on occupation class. Occupation classes 1 and 2 (office and light manual work) are accepted at standard rates. Class 3 occupations pay a 50% loading. Class 4 occupations are not accepted.

## 7. Claims
Medical expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the accident with original receipts and a medical report.
